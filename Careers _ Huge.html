Benefits.

  • Huge Holidays

    We pause together so we can recharge together. Enjoy company-wide extended holidays and long weekends throughout the year—because rest is part of the work.

  • Summer Fridays

    From the last Monday of May to the first Monday of September, we encourage you to log off early and take the extra time to kick off your summer weekends.

  • Flex Vacation Time

    Beyond our shared company-wide breaks, we empower you to take sufficient time to relax and focus. Making things that matter starts with giving people space for what matters most.

  • Sick and Safe Leave

    True wellness is both body and mind. We encourage you to take the downtime needed so you can recover and feel better.

  • The GOAT (Go Away Time)

    A decade of dedication is a milestone worth honoring and celebrating. Your 10th anniversary unlocks a one-month paid sabbatical to disconnect, reenergize, and focus entirely on life outside of work.

  • Life Essentials

    To support life outside the office, monthly subsidies for wellness and data plans keeps you connected and feeling your best—on and off the clock.
